Commercial structure

Two separate charges

Atlas separates the initial implementation work from the recurring software licence. Implementation and customisation are paid once, while continued use of Atlas is covered by the annual software licence.

First-year example
R85,000
R50,000 implementation + R35,000 annual licence for a college in the up-to-500-student tier.

Colleges move into the appropriate annual licence tier according to the student capacity covered by their Atlas licence.

Simple budgeting

Colleges know the annual licence amount associated with their selected student scale instead of paying for individual software actions.

Scales with the institution

The licence grows with the size of the college rather than applying one identical annual price to every institution.

Clear separation

Implementation and customisation are once-off professional services, while ongoing Atlas access is covered by the annual software licence.

Pricing Notes

  • The implementation, customisation and deployment fee is a once-off charge per college.
  • The annual software licence is recurring and is selected according to the applicable student-capacity tier.
  • Licence tiers are fixed annual amounts rather than per-transaction application credits.
  • The annual licence covers continued use of the Atlas platform and its configured admissions functionality during the applicable licence period.
  • College-specific work outside the agreed implementation scope may require a separate written quotation.
  • All prices are stated in South African Rand (ZAR).
