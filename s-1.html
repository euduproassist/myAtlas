Online Academic Application Process

To ensure a smooth and successful application, please follow the steps below. You will need to provide accurate and complete information and have your identification documents and academic records ready before you begin.

1 Complete the online application form.
2 Provide the required supporting documents.
3 Pay the application fee, if applicable.
4 Wait for your application to be reviewed.
5 Receive your admission decision via email.
6 Follow the next steps to register and enrol.

POPIA Privacy Notice

1. Introduction
Atlas College is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

This notice explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you apply to study at our college.

2. Information We Collect
We may collect personal details including your name, identity number, passport number, contact details, academic history and supporting documentation.

3. Purpose of Collection
Personal information is collected for the purpose of processing your application, communicating with you regarding your application, maintaining student records and fulfilling applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Protection of Information
Atlas College takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

5. Applicant Rights
Applicants may have rights relating to access to, correction of and protection of their personal information as provided for under applicable legislation.

6. Contact
If you have questions regarding the processing of your personal information, please contact the Atlas College admissions office.
