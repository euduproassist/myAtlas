Academic Application Process

NOTE: Welcome to our online application platform. We are excited that you have considered applying to our institution. Please read the guidelines below before proceeding:

Returning Applicants:

If you already possess an active student number, log directly into your student portal using your credentials.

New / First-Time Applicants:

  • Ensure you have a valid, active email address as all official institutional communications go through it.
  • Click the continuation action items on each section to commit and save your session data.
  • The application cycle is completely finalized only after executing the final submission confirmation step.
