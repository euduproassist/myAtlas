Atlas Institution Services

Institution Implementation & Customization

A complete institution-specific preparation and implementation service for organizations adopting the Atlas application management platform.

MEMORANDUM

Subject Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization
Fee R60,000 Once-Off
Service Type Mandatory Institution Implementation
Platform Atlas
01 — PURPOSE

Purpose of the Memorandum

This memorandum outlines the mandatory Institution Implementation & Customization service provided by Atlas following the successful completion of the institution's Atlas pilot and the institution's decision to proceed with the platform.

The service is designed to transform the standard Atlas platform into an institution-specific environment reflecting the institution's identity, programmes, application requirements, information, workflows and operational needs.

02 — SERVICE

What the Implementation Includes

The Institution Implementation & Customization package encompasses the preparation, configuration, customization, implementation, testing and staff enablement required for the institution's Atlas environment.

01

Institution Branding

Application of the institution's logo, colours, visual identity and other approved branding elements throughout the relevant Atlas environments.

02

Institution Information

Configuration of institutional information, descriptions, contact details, website links and other required content.

03

Programmes

Configuration of the institution's programmes, courses and associated application information.

04

Application Process

Configuration of the institution-specific application fields, requirements, workflow and application journey.

05

Communication

Customization of automated email communications and institutional messaging to reflect the institution's identity and communication standards.

06

Official Documents

Customization of acceptance letters and relevant institutional correspondence.

07

Staff Environment

Configuration and customization of the institution's staff-facing Atlas environment.

08

Administrative Environment

Configuration of the relevant administrative environment and institution-specific settings.

03 — REQUIREMENTS

Institution Information & Materials

Atlas will collect and review the information and materials required to complete the institution-specific implementation.

  • Official institution logo and approved branding assets.
  • Institutional colours and relevant visual identity information.
  • Programme and course information.
  • Application requirements and required applicant fields.
  • Institutional information and approved website content.
  • Relevant website links and contact information.
  • Email communication requirements and approved wording.
  • Acceptance letter requirements and approved institutional content.
  • Staff and administrative user requirements.
  • Other information reasonably required to configure the institution's Atlas environment.
04 — IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS

Implementation Process

The implementation process follows a structured sequence to ensure that the institution's Atlas environment is correctly prepared and aligned with the information supplied by the institution.

Stage 01

Requirements Collection

Collection and review of the institution's branding, programmes, application requirements, content, documents, links and other required information.

Stage 02

Implementation Planning

Review of the supplied information and preparation of the institution-specific Atlas configuration.

Stage 03

Customization

Application of the institution's identity, programmes, information, application process, communications, documents and relevant portal configurations.

Stage 04

Implementation

Deployment of the configured institution-specific Atlas environment and associated settings.

Stage 05

Testing & Corrections

Testing of the implemented environment, identification of required corrections and completion of necessary adjustments.

Stage 06

Staff Training

Training of designated institutional staff on the relevant Atlas functions and administrative processes.

05 — SCOPE

Mandatory Implementation Package

The services listed in this memorandum form one integrated implementation package. The institution is not required to select individual components from the package.

One Mandatory Implementation Package

The complete Institution Implementation & Customization service is provided as a single mandatory package and applies uniformly to each institution adopting Atlas.

06 — PROFESSIONAL FEE

Institution Implementation Fee

Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization

A once-off mandatory fee covering the complete institution-specific preparation, customization, implementation, testing and staff training described in this memorandum.

R60,000 Once-Off
Fixed Fee

The implementation fee is a fixed institutional fee and is not calculated according to individual features selected or an hourly rate.

07 — TERMS

Implementation Terms

  1. The Institution Implementation & Customization Fee is mandatory following the institution's decision to proceed with Atlas after the pilot.
  2. The fee covers the complete implementation package described in this memorandum.
  3. The implementation process is based on information and materials supplied or approved by the institution.
  4. Changes to requirements or requests that materially fall outside the implementation scope described in this memorandum may be treated as separate work.
  5. The institution is responsible for providing accurate and approved institutional information required for the implementation.
  6. Designated institutional staff are expected to participate in the relevant training and review activities.
  7. The implementation fee is separate from the applicable Atlas annual platform licence.
08 — ACCEPTANCE

Institutional Acknowledgement

By accepting the Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization package, the institution acknowledges that the services described in this memorandum constitute the institution-specific preparation and implementation required for its Atlas environment.

The institution further acknowledges the mandatory once-off implementation fee of R60,000.

Authorisation

The parties acknowledge and accept the terms outlined in this memorandum.
