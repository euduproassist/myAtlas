Official Memorandum
Atlas Institution Services

Institution Implementation & Customization

A complete institution-specific preparation, customization and implementation service for institutions adopting the Atlas application management platform.

MEMORANDUM

Subject Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization
Implementation Fee R60,000 Once-Off
Service Type Mandatory Institution Implementation
Platform Atlas
01 — Purpose

Purpose of the Memorandum

This memorandum outlines the mandatory Institution Implementation & Customization service provided by Atlas following the successful completion of the institution's Atlas pilot and the institution's decision to proceed with the platform.

The service is designed to prepare and configure Atlas specifically around the institution's identity, programmes, application requirements, institutional information, workflows and operational requirements.

02 — Scope

Institution-Specific Customization

The implementation package includes the preparation and customization of the Atlas environment using information, branding and requirements supplied or approved by the institution.

01

Institution Branding

Application of the institution's approved logo, colours, visual identity and branding elements throughout the relevant Atlas environments.

02

Institutional Information

Configuration of institutional information, descriptions, contact details, website links and other required content.

03

Programmes

Configuration of the institution's programmes, courses and associated application information.

04

Application Process

Configuration of institution-specific application fields, requirements, workflows and application processes.

05

Automated Communications

Customization of automated emails and institutional communications to reflect the institution.

06

Acceptance Letters

Customization of acceptance letters and relevant institutional correspondence.

07

Staff Portal

Configuration and customization of the institution's staff-facing Atlas environment.

08

Administrative Environment

Configuration of the institution's relevant administrative environment, settings and institutional information.

03 — Requirements

Information & Materials Required

Atlas will collect and review the information and materials required to complete the institution-specific implementation.

  • Official institution logo and approved branding assets.
  • Institutional colours and relevant visual identity information.
  • Programme and course information.
  • Application requirements and required applicant fields.
  • Institutional information and approved website content.
  • Relevant website links and institutional contact information.
  • Email communication requirements and approved wording.
  • Acceptance letter requirements and approved institutional content.
  • Staff and administrative user requirements.
  • Other information reasonably required for the institution-specific implementation.
04 — Process

Implementation Process

The implementation follows a structured process to ensure that the institution's Atlas environment is prepared according to the information and requirements supplied by the institution.

Stage 01

Requirements Collection

Collection and review of branding, programmes, application requirements, institutional content, documents, links and other required information.

Stage 02

Implementation Planning

Review of supplied information and preparation of the institution-specific Atlas configuration.

Stage 03

Customization

Application of the institution's branding, programmes, information, application process, communications, documents and portal settings.

Stage 04

Implementation

Implementation of the configured institution-specific Atlas environment.

Stage 05

Testing & Corrections

Testing of the implemented environment, identification of required corrections and completion of necessary adjustments.

Stage 06

Staff Training

Training of designated institutional staff on the relevant Atlas functions and administrative processes.

05 — Package

Mandatory Implementation Package

The services listed in this memorandum form one integrated implementation package. The institution does not select individual components from the package.

One Mandatory Package

The complete Institution Implementation & Customization service is provided as one mandatory package and applies uniformly to each institution adopting Atlas.

06 — Professional Fee

Institution Implementation Fee

Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization

A once-off mandatory fee covering the institution-specific preparation, customization, implementation, testing and staff training described in this memorandum.

R60,000 Once-Off
Fixed Institutional Fee

The implementation fee is a fixed institutional fee and is not calculated according to individual features selected or an hourly rate.

07 — Terms

Implementation Terms

  1. The Institution Implementation & Customization Fee is mandatory following the institution's decision to proceed with Atlas after the pilot.
  2. The fee covers the complete implementation package described in this memorandum.
  3. The implementation process is based on information and materials supplied or approved by the institution.
  4. Changes to requirements or requests that materially fall outside the implementation scope described in this memorandum may be treated as separate work.
  5. The institution is responsible for providing accurate and approved institutional information required for the implementation.
  6. Designated institutional staff are expected to participate in relevant training and review activities.
  7. The implementation fee is separate from the applicable Atlas annual platform licence.
08 — Acknowledgement

Institutional Acknowledgement

By accepting the Atlas Institution Implementation & Customization package, the institution acknowledges that the services described in this memorandum constitute the institution-specific preparation and implementation of its Atlas environment.

The institution further acknowledges the mandatory once-off implementation fee of R60,000.

Authorisation

The parties acknowledge and accept the terms outlined in this memorandum.
